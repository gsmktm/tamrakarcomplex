Produce a 45-second cinematic multi-scene advertising film for TamrakarComplex.com, the New Road, Kathmandu electronics discovery, buy/sell/exchange and repair platform. Photorealistic premium tech advertising, authentic Nepali environments and people, coherent equipment design, subtle animated digital light traces, lens flares and deliberate pacing. Palette midnight navy **#071629** and vivid orange **#FF681D**.

**Production target:** 3840×2160 widescreen and 2160×3840 vertical, each independently composed and reviewed. **No fake website screenshots:** capture actual approved TamrakarComplex.com browser screens and composite them in postproduction. Use official logo and approved visuals only. Nepali female or male professional narration using `captions-ne.srt` as the final reviewed script. Licensed original electronic score with appropriate ducking; deliver clean voice, music, subtitles and full mix stems if possible.

Camera dives down toward the phones and customers; a gentle neon orange light connects the streets to a digital grid. On-screen: **NEW ROAD, KATHMANDU** and **THE HEART OF NEPAL'S TECH MARKET**. Precise studio closeups of phones, tablet, laptop, watch and wireless earbuds, with elegant parallax. Then actual website category and comparison screen capture. On-screen **DISCOVER • COMPARE • EXPLORE**; do not invent prices, ratings, devices, stock or discounts.

### 04 / 19–29s · Physical services connect
Three seamless vignettes: authentic customer browses a store directory; a shopper asks about exchanging an existing phone; technician professionally inspects smartphone at repair bench. Orange line bridges the three. On-screen **FIND STORES**, **SELL & EXCHANGE**, **REPAIR SERVICES**. No unverified 'certified' claim.

Warm Nepali Tihar-inspired diyas, marigold garlands, respectful family celebration and subtle electronics gift presentation. Integrate site festival banner as a real UI capture. On-screen **FESTIVAL TECH COLLECTIONS** and **DISCOVER SOMETHING SPECIAL**. No invented sale prices, savings percentages, free gifts or coupon codes.

### 06 / 36–45s · Brand signature
A connected digital orange network of devices and local shops on midnight navy. Camera eases out to official logo, orange CTA, and **tamrakarcomplex.com**. On-screen **YOUR TECH. YOUR MARKETPLACE.** and **FROM NEW ROAD TO A SMARTER TOMORROW.**. Land on an original two-note brand sonic signature.
