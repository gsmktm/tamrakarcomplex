# TAMRAKAR COMPLEX — 45-second cinematic promotion

## Purpose
A premium technology marketplace ad for **tamrakarcomplex.com**, New Road, Kathmandu, Nepal.

**Primary:** vertical 9:16  |  **Alternative:** 16:9  |  **Master target:** 4K UHD  |  **Timing:** 45 seconds.

### Creative treatment
Palette: #071629 midnight navy / #FF681D orange / silver, white, and warm festival gold. Film language: plausible Kathmandu market footage, macro gadget silhouettes, flowing digital orange lines, kinetic on-screen captions, and dignified Tihar cultural imagery. Music: licensed original future-electronic score. Narrator: articulate Nepali voice, not an avatar. Use the **official logo and recorded website screen capture** when available; do not hallucinate merchant photos or site functionality.
